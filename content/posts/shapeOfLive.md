of my discomfort and end up in a set of actions that can help me.

1. I developed or stay in some kind of wordless thinking, which makes difficult to speak and more over connect ideas.
2. I am not organized with my thinking, that can be appreciated though other aspect of my life, like how I organize my room.

1. Tell the truth.
2. Be carefull with my words, everey single word counts. It is how a give shape to my life.
